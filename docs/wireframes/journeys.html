What drawing the flows decided

Proposed decisions

These came up while walking through real cases. None of them are in the brief yet. Confirm or push back, and I'll add them.

NavigationThree tabs: Today, Plan, ProgressPlan holds Missions and Later Actions that don't belong to a Mission. Focus, Rebuild and Close the day are full-screen moments, not tabs.
TodayThe first Today Action becomes the Main ActionNobody has to choose a Main Action before they can start. It can be changed with one tap.
TodayAn empty Today suggests each Mission's next ActionThis links Missions to Today without a separate planning step. One tap puts the suggestion on Today.
FocusThe timer counts up, with an optional targetA countdown that runs out reads as failing. Counting up matches "record actual time". The target can be 25, 50 or 90 minutes, or the Action's estimate.
DayA new day starts at 4:00, not midnightSomeone working at 00:30 is still in their day. The overnight rule runs at 4:00.
AddingWhere you add something decides where it goesAdding from Today puts it on Today. Adding from Plan or a Mission puts it in Later. No extra choice is needed.
TodayCompleted Actions stay visible, struck throughThey sink to the bottom of Today until the day ends. That's the evidence of the day, without a stats card.
Close the dayAppears at the bottom of Today after 18:00A quiet row, not a notification or a pop-up. It also appears once every Today Action is done.
RebuildA small day has no scoreItems are ticked off, but nothing says "2 of 3". One done is a good small day.
Journey 1

First run

Goal: the first real Action in about 60 seconds, with no sign-up, tour or Mission.

Journey 2

A normal day

Goal: open, know what to do and start. Close the day in the evening if you feel like it.

Journey 3

A hard day

Goal: turn "too much" into one to three small things, and make that feel like a good day.

Journey 4

Coming back after three weeks

Goal: working again within two taps, with no guilt and nothing lost.

Journey 5

Creating a Mission

Goal: a goal with a first step in under a minute, and no setup screens.

Still open

Questions for you

  1. Does Progress need its own tab? Without points and rank it's light: this week, the all-time record, and per-Mission investment. It could live at the top of Plan instead, which would leave only two tabs. I lean towards keeping the tab, so progress has a home that isn't Today.
  2. Is Focus allowed without an Action? I'd say no. If the user taps Focus with nothing selected, they type a name first. Every minute is recorded against something, which keeps Mission investment honest.
  3. Are the Rebuild slot names right? "Something that matters / For your body / For your space" is my draft. Your words for these matter more than mine.
